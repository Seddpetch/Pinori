# 🌷 Pinori

**Your places, your adventures, your little corner of the world.**

Pinori is a map-first travel and place-saving app designed for people who are always collecting places they want to visit.

Ever saved a restaurant from TikTok, screenshotted a cute café on Instagram, or added somewhere to a never-ending list of places to visit?

We've all been there.

Pinori brings all those little discoveries together in one place, helping you organise your saved spots, plan adventures, and turn your growing wishlist into real-life experiences. 🗺️

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌸 Currently available

- 📍 **Save Places:** Create and save places to your personal collection.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Map:** Explore saved locations using an interactive map.
- 🧳 **Trip Management:** Create, organise, edit and manage trips.
- 🏷️ **Place Categories:** Organise places into categories such as cafés, restaurants, shopping, attractions, museums and more.
- ✏️ **Place Editing:** Update place information and manage saved locations.
- 💾 **Local Persistence:** Keep your saved places and trip information stored locally.
- 🤖 **AI Place Extraction:** Extract useful place information from shared content, links, screenshots and text.
- 💗 **Visited Status:** Keep track of places you've already explored.
- 🔎 **Place Discovery:** Find and organise your saved locations.

### 🪄 Coming soon

We're constantly working on making Pinori even better!

- ✨ **AI Itinerary Generation:** Generate personalised itineraries using your saved places.
- 🧭 **Smart Place Grouping:** Automatically group nearby locations to make trip planning easier.
- 📅 **Day-by-Day Planning:** Organise trips into individual days.
- 🎨 **Custom Map Themes:** Personalise your map with different visual styles.
- 🖼️ **Custom Trip Covers:** Give every adventure its own look and personality.
- 📤 **Exportable Itineraries:** Export your travel plans and saved places.
- 🤝 **Collaborative Maps:** Plan adventures and share collections with other people.
- 🌍 **Creator-Curated Maps:** Discover collections and recommendations from other travellers.
- 🔗 **Improved Social Imports:** Make importing saved places from social platforms even easier.
- 🌐 **Web Experience:** Access your saved places and trips beyond the mobile app.
